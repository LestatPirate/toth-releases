## English
**Toth does not collect, send or share personal data.** It runs entirely on your device.
- **No network:** the app makes no internet connections and has no accounts, telemetry, usage analytics or ads.
- **What is stored, only on your Mac:** your captures (by default in `~/Pictures/Capture`) and preferences (shortcut, language, last customization of each tool).
- **Screen recording permission:** macOS asks for it so the app can capture the screen. It is used only when you request a capture, and the image never leaves your device.
- **Third-party content:** images you capture may contain other people's data. You decide what to capture and share, and you must comply with applicable law.
- **Your rights (GDPR):** since the app does not process personal data on the Owner's behalf, there is no data of yours for us to access, correct or delete. Questions: [CONTACT EMAIL]. You may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD).
